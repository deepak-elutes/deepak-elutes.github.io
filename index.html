I am Deepak Timalsina, PhD candidate of Bioanalytical Chemistry in Wang Research group at University of Kansas Department of Chemistry. My work develops the ultra-trace methods to measure PFAS, also called "forever chemicals", and develop the platforms that measure smallest molecules, at concentrations most instruments miss.
